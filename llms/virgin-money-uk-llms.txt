# Virgin Money UK

> Virgin Money UK is a full-service UK retail and business bank (Virgin Money, Clydesdale Bank, Yorkshire Bank, and B brands), acquired by Nationwide Building Society on 1 October 2024. As an FCA/PRA-authorised ASPSP it publishes UK Open Banking (OBIE Read/Write Standard) APIs — Account & Transaction Information (AIS), Payment Initiation (PIS), and Confirmation of Funds (CBPII) — plus a public Open Data API. Access to the Read/Write APIs is secured with FAPI OAuth2/OpenID Connect, mutual-TLS, PSD2 strong customer authentication, and dynamic client registration using OBIE (OBWAC/OBSeal) or eIDAS (QWAC/QSEAL) certificates.

## APIs

- [Open Data API](https://developer.virginmoney.com/) — public ATM/branch locator + product data (OBIE Open Data Standard).
- [Account & Transaction API (AIS)](https://developer.virginmoney.com/merged/accounts/) — accounts, balances, transactions, standing orders, direct debits, statements. OBIE v3.1.x.
- [Domestic / Scheduled / International / File Payments (PIS)](https://developer.virginmoney.com/merged/dip/) — payment initiation. OBIE v3.1.x.
- [Confirmation of Funds (CBPII)](https://developer.virginmoney.com/merged/cof/) — funds availability check. OBIE v3.1.x.
- [Token API](https://developer.virginmoney.com/merged/token/) — OAuth2 token endpoint (v3.0).
- [Dynamic Client Registration (DCR)](https://developer.virginmoney.com/merged/dynamic-registration/) — TPP registration (v3.2).

## Auth

- Profile: FAPI (Financial-grade API), UK Open Banking / OBIE Read-Write.
- Issuer: https://api.prod.ob.virginmoney.com/vmpsd2-psd2prod/psd2-production
- Scopes: openid, accounts, payments, fundsconfirmations
- Flows: authorization_code (consent) + client_credentials; mutual-TLS certificate-bound tokens; PSD2 SCA (acr urn:openbanking:psd2:sca).
